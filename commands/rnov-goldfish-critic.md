# /rnov-goldfish-critic

Follow the `review` skill, Goldfish protocol (document review) section.

## Steps

1. Follow the `review` skill, Goldfish protocol, stage 3 (Critic), over the feature range.
2. Run the lenses in parallel and synthesise them; do not let one lens's clean bill hide another's finding.
3. Cover architecture, scalability, cost and quota, faulty assumptions, unmitigated risks, heading structure and mandatory sections, fluff and jargon.

## Output

Findings ordered by severity, each with evidence from the document.
