# /rnov-blind-doubt

Follow the `review` skill, Blind doubt review for risky decisions section.

## Steps

1. Follow the `review` skill, Blind doubt review section. The reviewer receives the artifact and the contract only — never the author's claim, reasoning, or confidence.
2. Classify findings as contract misread, valid and actionable, trade-off, or noise; the author answers each class differently.
3. One round by default; escalate to a stronger or cross-family reviewer by stakes, not ritually.

## Output

Classified findings with the author's answer per class.
