# /rnov-goldfish-comprehension

Follow the `review` skill, Goldfish protocol (document review) section.

## Steps

1. Follow the `review` skill, Goldfish protocol, stage 2 (Comprehension).
2. Read only the target file, the files it references, and an optional baseline reading list — never the conversation that produced the document.
3. Restate the feature in your own words, then list every place you had to guess, assume, or could not tell.
4. Undefined terms, unexplained acronyms, assumed prerequisites, and vague explanations are findings, not style notes.

## Output

Restatement plus the list of guess points, each naming its section.
