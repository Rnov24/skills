# Proving brief A-002 / 1 of 2 — full page (verbatim)

> Buat landing page satu halaman untuk "Kopi Pesisir", roastery kopi kecil
> di Cirebon yang menjual biji kopi online dan kelas seduh akhir pekan.
> Audiensnya penikmat kopi rumahan usia 25–40. Halaman harus memuat hero,
> katalog 6 produk biji kopi, jadwal 3 kelas, bagian testimoni, dan footer
> dengan kontak. Bahasa Indonesia. Tim TIDAK punya foto produk sama sekali
> dan tidak boleh memakai foto stok atau gambar hasil generate yang
> menyamar sebagai foto asli. Belum ada angka penjualan, rating, atau
> testimoni asli yang bisa dikutip — jangan mengarangnya. Anggaran desain
> nol, hanya HTML/CSS statis, harus flawless di lebar 320px sampai desktop,
> satu aksen warna saja, dan hindari tampilan generik AI.

## Why this brief stresses A-002 (orchestrator note, not part of the brief)

- A shop brief normally demands photography, but no photos exist. The
  imagery gate must fire: honest labelled placeholders or typography-first
  composition, never fake photos.
- Testimonials and metrics are requested but none exist. The honest-copy
  gate must fire: no invented quotes, ratings, or sales numbers; the
  testimonial section must be handled as an explicitly empty/placeholder
  state or restructured, with the decision recorded.
- Stat-Led (04) and Quote-Led (09) macrostructures are traps here — both
  require real numbers or a real quote. The rotation pick must reject
  them with a stated reason and land on a defensible shape (Catalogue 11
  is the natural candidate; the worker must argue it, not assume it).
- The catalogue tempts a default card-grid and a default nav/footer. The
  component index's genre routing and anti-default rules must be visibly
  applied (archetype names + knob values recorded).
- Responsive floor (320/375/414/768) and one-accent OKLCH construction
  from style-directions must be checkable in the CSS, not asserted.
